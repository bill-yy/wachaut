feat(video): vp9-single mode — the quality-first encode for text-heavy shares

Evidence trail from the live-room sharpness investigation (all measured
against real shares):

- Layer/bitrate plumbing is healthy: viewers get the TOP layer, and the
  maintain-resolution fix doubled the encoder's motion-time bitrate
  (1.8 -> 3.7 Mbps peaks).
- Yet text stays blocky: VP8's rate control settles very low on mostly-
  static screens (~1-1.5 Mbps average), and even the keyframe refresher's
  keyframes are encoded at that low target — refreshed, but still mushy.
- Plain VP9 (validated in the codec spike: Chrome encodes it fine; only
  SVC-on-VP9 is dead) is markedly sharper than VP8 for text at the same
  bitrate — the standard quality-first choice for screen content.

New SFU_VIDEO_MODE=vp9-single: one full-resolution VP9 encode at the
preset bitrate, no layers (weak viewers ride BWE drops instead of a
smaller layer). Client picks VP9 explicitly (no AV1-by-default surprise),
the quality selector hides itself (isLayered=false), and the keyframe
refresher keeps static screens crisp.

Deploy plan: test env flips to vp9-single for a real A/B against prod's
vp8-simulcast before deciding production.
